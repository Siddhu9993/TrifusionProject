01
Healthcare

MedAxis – Complete Healthcare Automation & Telemedicine System

“ MedAxis operated a multi-department hospital where patient records were maintained in physical files that moved manually between OPD, pharmacy, and billing counters. Doctors had no access to patient history during consultations without physically retrieving folders. Finance, pharmacy, and clinical teams operated in complete silos — a prescription written by a doctor required a human runner to reach the pharmacy. Billing errors were frequent due to manual charge compilation. The hospital had no telemedicine capability, losing a significant share of rural and follow-up patients who couldn't visit in person. Leadership had no dashboard visibility into bed occupancy, doctor utilization, or revenue per department. ”

02
EdTech

Ba Schools – ERP & Learning Management Platform for Modern Education

“ Ba Schools, a growing chain of K–12 institutions, was operating entirely on manual processes — attendance was recorded in paper registers, exam scores were compiled in spreadsheets, and fee collection was tracked through handwritten ledgers. Teachers had no centralized system to assign or review homework, and parents received performance updates only during quarterly PTMs. The school administration spent 3–4 hours daily on data reconciliation across departments. There was no visibility for school leadership on academic performance trends, fee defaults, or teacher productivity. As the institution expanded to multiple branches, the lack of a unified system created inconsistencies in operations and increased the risk of data loss. ”
